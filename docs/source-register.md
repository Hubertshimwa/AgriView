## Measurement rules

- NISR farmer-practice percentages are shares of farmers, not shares of land or plots.
- Agricultural land in hectares is a land-use measure. It is not equivalent to harvested crop area.
- Production is metric tonnes. Yield is tonnes per hectare.
- Coffee export tonnes and revenue refer to exports, not national farm production.
- NAEB tea values describe made tea and use reporting periods different from the NISR SAS crop table.
- If a crop value is absent from a chosen release, show a data gap and source link. Do not use zero or interpolate.
